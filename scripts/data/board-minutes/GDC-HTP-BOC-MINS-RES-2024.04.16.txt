I. Call to Order
The public meeting was called to order by Co-Chair Glen at approximately 12:00 PM.
Co-Chair Glen advised that public comments were solicited virtually and audio of the meeting was
being live-streamed from the Gateway Program website. 
Co-Chair Glen noted that the Commission was continuing to solicit virtual comments, and that a 
form for soliciting these comments was posted on the Gateway website for those who wished to submit 
a comment for today’s meeting. 
Acting Secretary Caulfield conducted a roll call and confirmed that all Commissioners were 
present, and there was a quorum.  
Acting Secretary Caulfield stated that adequate notice of today’s regular public meeting of the 
Board of Commissioners of the Commission had been provided in accordance with the Commission’s 
enabling legislation. 
II. Report on Minutes of the Feb
 ruary 28, 2024 Meeting
Acting Secretary Caulfield reported that copies of the Minutes of the meeting of February 28,
2024 were delivered in electronic form  by Acting Secretary Caulfield  to the Governors of New York and 
New Jersey on February 29, 2024 and that the time for action by the Governors of New York and New 
Jersey expired at midnight on March 14, 2024, at which time the actions recorded in the Minutes went 
into full force and effect. 
III. Gateway Program Update
The Commission’s Chief Executive Officer, Kris Kolluri,  provided updates on the Hudson Tunnel
Project (“HTP”).  He stated that critical third party agreements were submitted to the FTA on March 30, 
2024 to the Federal Transit Administration which was a major milestone to enter into a Full Funding 
Agreement and secure funding for the HTP by June 2024.  He also provided a construction update on the 
Hudson Yards Concrete Casing, Section 3 and Tonnelle Avenue projects.   
Patrick McCoy , Acting Chief Financial Officer, presented the audited financial statements 
prepared by Deloitte & Touche LLP as of December 31, 2023 for approval by the GDC Board of 
Commissioners in accordance with the GDC Act.
