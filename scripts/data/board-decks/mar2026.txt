For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only
GDC Lawsuit
5 For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only
A hearing on GDC’s lawsuit against the federal government in 
the U.S. Court of Federal Claims took place on March 12.
Key Outcomes:
➢ The court found that the federal government had breached its 
contracts with GDC by withholding funding.
➢ The court dismissed GDC’s claims related to the $205 million the 
government withheld from October through February because 
those funds have been paid thanks to the separate lawsuit brought 
by the states of New Jersey and New York.
➢ The court will provide expedited review of similar claims if they 
arise in the future.  
As expected, the court did not yet rule on GDC’s two claims seeking 
to recover damages related to the suspension of work in February.

Integrity Monitoring Services
15 For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only
In line with GDC’s commitment to being a responsible steward 
of taxpayer dollars, we are seeking the Board's approval of 
contracts for integrity monitoring services with:
➢ DeLuca Advisory & Consulting Services, LLC
➢ CohnReznick Advisory, LLC and Guidepost Solutions, LLC (JV)
Integrity Monitoring is an FTA best practice that will provide 
independent fraud and efficiency oversight at all GDC 
construction sites and ensure full and transparent compliance 
with applicable laws and requirements.
These monitors’ work will reinforce GDC’s existing procedures, 
safeguards, and controls for the Hudson Tunnel Project.
