Track Record of Successful Project Delivery
GDC has worked hand-in-hand with MPA to: 
➢ Secure full funding for the Hudson Tunnel Project.
➢ Award contracts for three construction packages, 
including the first tunnel boring project.
➢ Progress the Tonnelle Avenue Project toward completion 
on schedule and on budget. 
➢ Procure and manufacture two tunnel boring machines 
for the Palisades Tunnel Project.
➢ Advance procurement of two additional construction 
packages that will be awarded in the coming months.
