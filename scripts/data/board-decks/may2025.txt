The Hudson Yards Concrete Casing 
project is preserving the rail right-
of-way beneath Hudson Yards
➢ Hudson Yards is the only location that 
provides appropriate space for the 
new tunnel to be fully integrated with 
existing Penn Station tracks and 
platforms
➢ Sections 1 and 2 of the concrete casing 
were completed in 2018 
➢ HYCC-3 is the final link that will 
connect to the new tunnel under the 
Hudson River
4
Construction Milestone: HYCC-3 Project 50% Complete
For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only


=== PAGE BREAK ===

Various factors make HYCC-3 a 
challenging project
➢ Construction is taking place in a dense, 
urban environment
➢ This section of the concrete casing 
passes under the High Line
➢ Proximity to skyscrapers and 
underground infrastructure
Construction progress to date:
➢ 337 secant piles driven to support the 
walls of the trench
➢ 40,600 tons of soil and more than 1 
million gallons of water removed
➢ 54 struts installed in the trench

Tonnelle Avenue Bridge & Utility Relocation
Anticipated Completion: Q4 2025 | Jobs Created: 420
 The Tonnelle Ave. Project will create the access 
point for the new tunnel tracks and the tunnel 
boring machines that will build the New Jersey 
section of the new tunnel. 
For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only
Soil and rock excavation of the center 
portion of the bridge is in progress.
Superstructure work, installation of concrete box beams, and bridge 
deck construction are in progress in the middle portion of the bridge. 

=== PAGE BREAK ===

Hudson River Ground Stabilization (HRGS)
The HRGS Project is mixing concrete into the 
soil beneath the Hudson River to reinforce 
the riverbed in preparation for tunnel boring. 
Anticipated Completion: Q3 2027Anticipated Completion: Q3 2027 | Jobs Created: 2,270
For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only
Mixing is taking place within a temporary cofferdam to shield 
the work area from the current and protect the river ecosystem. 
The team is mixing 
concrete into the 
soil in columns. 200 
columns have been 
mixed to date. 

=== PAGE BREAK ===

Palisades Tunnel Project
Anticipated Completion: Q4 2027
The Palisades Tunnel Project will construct the first mile of 
twin-tunnels on the New Jersey side of the Hudson River.
Tunnel Boring Machines are in 
production, and tunnel boring 
is expected to launch next year.
Early work, including pile driving and installation 
of monitoring instruments, is in progress. 
Anticipated Completion: Q4 2027 | Jobs Created: 5,500
Photo provided by Herrenknecht
For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only
Crews will soon begin construction 
of a noise wall at the site where the 
Hudson County Shaft will be built.

Manhattan Tunnel Project
GDC visited the site with 
the contractor in March.
Anticipated Completion: Q4 2029
The Manhattan Tunnel Project will build the portion of the tunnel 
from the Manhattan bulkhead to the Hudson Yards Concrete Casing.
The MTP requires 
complex tunnelling to 
navigate historic fill 
and utility lines. The 
team will use a digger 
shield to navigate 
these obstacles.
Anticipated Completion: Q4 2029 | Jobs Created: 5,800
