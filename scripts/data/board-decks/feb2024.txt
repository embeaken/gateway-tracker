Why a Delivery Partner
5
• Innovative model that has worked internationally for public agencies to 
deliver large-scale infrastructure projects. 
• Provides the technical, financial and compliance expertise required for 
federal grants.
• Provides necessary staffing, including Subject Matter Experts, to execute 
the Hudson Tunnel Project. 
For Indicative, Illustrative, Contemplative Purposes Only

=== PAGE BREAK ===

6
Delivery Partner Functions
• To meet the Hudson Tunnel Project’s (HTP) objectives, and build out GDC’s technical, 
financial, and compliance support capacities, the agency is bringing on board a Delivery 
Partner. 
• The Delivery Partner (DP) will provide expertise and support in delivering and managing the 
HTP by leveraging industry’s technical capability, delivery and execution experience, and 
personnel to achieve significant cost and time savings.
• It will provide GDC Project Management services in the form of staff augmentation, 
interface risk management, cost and schedule assurance, oversight, safety and quality 
management, innovation and value engineering, among others. 
• It will also provide SEPs with Construction Management services for specific HTP packages 
in the form of field management and safety, quality and compliance support, among others.

Delivery Partner Contract Award
7
Delivery Partner Services Contract: GDC-24-005-HTP
 Three shortlisted teams submitted proposals during the Request for Proposals stage of the procurement led by 
Amtrak on behalf of GDC.
 Selected Proposer Team: MPA Delivery Partners (MPA) - Comprised of Mace North America Limited, Parsons 
Transportation Group of New York, Inc., and Arcadis of New York, Inc.)
 Amtrak issued a Notice of Intent to Award on February 14, 2024.
 GDC to Award an initial 6-year contract ending in 2030, with 3 subsequent 3-year renewal options.
 Award Phase A of the contract in an amount not to exceed $26,823,778, as well as an additional amount equal 
to five percent (5%) for contingencies.
 Future Phases will be subject to approval by the GDC Board of Commissioners.

10
RRIF Loan Funding Agreements
• The local share of the HTP will be provided by the States of New York and New Jersey,
and the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey ("PANYNJ").
• GDC intends to enter into one or more loan agreements with the United States 
Department of Transportation (“USDOT”) under the Railroad Rehabilitation and 
Improvement Financing (“RRIF”) program administered by the USDOT (the “RRIF Loan 
Agreements”).
• The RRIF Loan Agreements will be supported by back-to-back payments made 
pursuant to three agreements that GDC will enter into with each respective local 
funding partner - State of New York, State of New Jersey and PANYNJ (each, a “RRIF 
Local Funding Agreement”).
